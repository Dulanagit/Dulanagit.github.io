- The design should be modern and professional

- The look should be modern, clean and eyecatching. 
- The sections should be there sequentially.
- If the content is too long in a section, hide the extra content and use a show more button or text to expand the rest. This way we can make the website userfriendly. 
- The website should be moduler and flexible. Easy to update and modify.
